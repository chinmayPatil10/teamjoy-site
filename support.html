Support

How can we help?

Most answers are below. If yours isn't, write us a message and a person will reply within 2 business days.

Getting started

  • Install: use the install link you were sent when your pilot application was approved, from a Slack account with a work email address.
  • Set up your team: open Teamjoy in Slack and use the Home tab to add members, choose a channel and set quiet hours.
  • Your own details: type /team me to add a birthday and preferences.
  • Ask for things: message Teamjoy, mention it in a channel, or type /team followed by a sentence.

Common questions

Why was my install refused?

Teamjoy is for teams at work, so it asks for a Slack account that uses a company email address.

Why didn't Teamjoy post my message to the channel?

Anything that goes to the whole team waits for the manager to press Approve.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Teamjoy sends one reminder per person per thing, and holds messages during your team's quiet hours.

Can Teamjoy take payments for a gift?

No. People pay the organiser directly, through the organiser's own PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Zelle or other link.

How do I remove Teamjoy?

Remove the app from your Slack workspace's app settings. Your team's data is then deleted.
