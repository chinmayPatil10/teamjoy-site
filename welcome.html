Welcome to the Founding Teams

Thanks for joining the pilot. Adding Teamjoy to your Slack takes about a minute.

1. Add Teamjoy to Slack

Use a Slack account with your work email address. Your workspace may ask an admin to approve the app.

Add to Slack

Slack will say this app is not yet in its Marketplace. That is expected during the pilot.

2. Set up your team

Open Teamjoy in Slack and use the Home tab to add your team members, choose a channel and set quiet hours.

3. Ask for something

Message Teamjoy: "Plan a team lunch", or "Any birthdays coming up?". Nothing reaches your team until you press Approve.

Need a hand?

Write us a message, or see the support page.
