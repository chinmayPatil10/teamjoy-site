Private by design

Built to know as little as possible

A teammate people trust has to keep things to itself. These rules are built into the product, not just promised.

You approve what goes out

Nothing is posted to the team, and nothing involving money happens, until the manager says yes.

Messages aren't stored

Teamjoy reads a conversation only to answer it. Slack messages and calendars are never kept.

Needs stay anonymous

Dietary and accessibility needs reach an event's organiser as counts, never with names.

Surprises stay surprises

The person a gift is for is never messaged about it and can't see it.

No nagging

One reminder per person per thing, and none during your team's quiet hours.

Birthdays without the year

Day and month only. Teamjoy never asks how old anyone is.

Secret Santa stays secret

Your holidays, your call

Questions

What managers ask first

Does Teamjoy read my team's messages?

Only the conversation it is asked to answer, at that moment, and it keeps none of it. It does not read your other channels. What Teamjoy stores

Does it hold our money?

No. For a group gift, people pay the organiser directly through the organiser's own PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or Zelle. Teamjoy only keeps the tally.

Can it post to my team without asking me?

No. Anything that goes to the whole team waits for you to press Approve.

Who sees dietary and accessibility needs?

Only the organiser of an event you say you're coming to, as counts without names. In a small team a count can still point to one person, so share only what you're comfortable with.

Who can see the Secret Santa pairs?

Nobody. Each person is told privately who they are buying for. The manager can't see the list, and it is deleted the day after the swap.

Does Teamjoy assume which holidays we celebrate?

No. It suggests work moments such as a year-end party or a quarterly happy hour. Religious and national holidays are off until the manager switches them on.

Are the games you suggest free?

Some are free and some are paid, and each is labelled. They are on other companies' websites, so the price is theirs. Teamjoy isn't paid to suggest any of them.

What happens after 60 days?

You choose. Keep Teamjoy for $9 a month per team, or stop, and nothing is charged. Pilot terms

How do I remove it?

Remove the app in your Slack workspace's settings. Your team's data is then deleted. More help
