Founding Teams pilot

Pilot terms

The Founding Teams pilot is open to 15 teams. Your team uses Teamjoy free for 60 days from the day you install it. In return we ask for honest feedback, through a short weekly check-in or a shared channel, and we may ask to quote you, which we will only do with your permission. You get help directly from the Teamjoy team throughout. After 60 days you choose: keep Teamjoy at $9 a month per team (or $7 a month billed yearly), or stop, in which case nothing is charged and you can remove the app at any time. Teamjoy is still young, so things will change and occasionally break; we will tell you when they do. How we handle your team's data is set out on the privacy page.
